📖 How to Play

🃏
1

Get 5 Trait Cards

You and your opponent each receive 5 random trait cards. These are YOUR animal's features!

🎯
2

Keep 3

Choose the 3 traits you think will help you survive the most challenges. Tap cards to select them.

🌪️
3

Face a Challenge

A wild event appears — a blizzard, a flood, a predator! Both players secretly pick one trait.

🤜🤛
4

Reveal Together

Both players tap REVEAL at the same time. Your hidden picks flip over — no changing your mind!

🏆
5

Score & Survive

🌟 Strong = 3 pts ⚡ Partial = 1 pt 💀 Weak = 0 pts
Play 4 rounds. Highest score wins!

💬
6

Talk About It

After the game, discuss why some traits worked and others didn't. Science thinking!

💡 Online Play Tip: Sit side by side on the same device, or one player holds the phone for their turn. Both tap REVEAL at the same time — no peeking at each other's screens!

🧑‍🏫 Educator's Guide

🔬 Science Concepts

  • Structural adaptation — body parts that aid survival (🐾 cards)
  • Behavioural adaptation — actions that aid survival (🧠 cards)
  • Environmental fit — traits only help in the right habitat
  • Trade-offs — keeping 3 of 5 mirrors real biological constraints
  • Natural selection — better-matched players score more = survive

💬 Discussion Prompts

  • Which trait helped in more than one challenge?
  • Did a strong trait suddenly become weak? What changed?
  • What would you swap — and what would you give up?
  • Why can't one animal be perfect at everything?

📅 5-Lesson Campaign

1What is an adaptation? → Play one game, sort cards into structural/behavioural
2Environmental fit → Replay with fixed desert challenges, compare outcomes
3Trade-offs → Choose only 2 traits; debate which 2 are best
4Natural selection → Play 3 games; tally which traits win most often
5Design an organism → Draw and name a creature; present to class

🚀 Extension Ideas

  • Draw your animal: Sketch the creature with its 3 traits
  • Habitat switch: Play challenges in Arctic, desert, rainforest
  • Invent a trait: What challenge is it strong against? Weak against?
📚 Curriculum Links: UK KS2 Science — Living things and their habitats · Evolution and inheritance. Aligned with NGSS Life Science LS4.
